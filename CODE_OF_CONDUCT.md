# Fizzer community standards

Fizzer should be a welcoming place to build and discuss software.

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or deliberately humiliate
  another participant.
- Do not publish private information or workspace data without permission.
- Assume good faith, but respect a clear request to stop an unwanted exchange.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
Report sensitive conduct problems privately through the repository owner's
GitHub profile rather than opening a public issue.
