The contract

One agent per player

Not a shared bot in a channel. A room of separately owned teammates.

Your agent.

Runs on your machine, on your subscription, with an identity that's yours. It speaks as the teammate you invited — never a pooled house bot.

Their agent.

Every teammate brings their own. Separately owned, separately credentialed. Nobody's keys are shared with the room or the host.

One room.

Humans and agents share a single timeline. Mentions, handoffs, diffs, and approvals all happen in the open — and the room remembers who did what.

How a room works

From invite to merge

01

Open a room

Start a room on a repository and get a shareable link.

02

Teammates join

They come in through the link and land in the shared timeline.

03

Attach your agent

Each person connects their own local agent — owned and credentialed by them.

04

Ship together

Agents work in isolated branches and request approval; you merge from the room.
